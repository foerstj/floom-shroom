# floom-shroom

A little mod for Dungeon Siege LoA.

Find a Red Spotted Floom Shroom, pick it up and eat it by right-clicking - and you'll turn into a mushroom yourself!

Not a human, not an animal, not quite a plant either. Feel connected to the endless network of mycelium. Just be. With no expectations. No stress, no worries, just limitlessness.

There are two kinds of Red Spotted Floom Shroom, the normal and the giant ones. The normal ones let you turn into a mushroom for one ingame day (about 38 real minutes), the giant ones let you turn into a mushroom forever.\
Of course, you can always exit the transformed state by clicking the untransform button.
